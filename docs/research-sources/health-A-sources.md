# Health batch A sources (fetched 2026-10-02)

Generator: build_health_A.py (same folder). PubMed abstracts were read through NCBI E-utilities efetch for the same PMID. validatebp.org, withings.com, quality-supplements.org, info.nsf.org, gardenoflife.com and ods.od.nih.gov were read in Chrome because they block plain fetches. DSLD label records were read via the NIH DSLD API (the human dsld.od.nih.gov pages return 403 to scripts).

## Hearing

### Shared
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know : OTC aids for adults 18+ with perceived mild to moderate loss, limited max output, red-flag symptoms, return policies not mandated.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42307981/ : De Sousa 2026 meta-analysis, 15 studies / 774 participants, no significant differences self-fit vs professional, low certainty.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40372746/ : Wu 2025 RCT, 245 adults, AUD scored 0.32 to 0.33 higher than OTC/OTC+, high vs low-end similar.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38990557/ : De Sousa 2024, 8-month follow-up, no significant APHAB/IOI-HA difference; hearX fee disclosures.
- https://lexiehearing.com/ : shutdown notice (assignment for benefit of creditors July 15, 2026; service ends Oct 22, 2026). Reason Lexie excluded.
- https://eargo.com/ : same wind-down notice, app and support end Oct 22, 2026. Reason Eargo excluded.
- https://www.hearingtracker.com/otc-hearing-aids/full-list : used only to identify candidates (not cited).
- openFDA 510(k) API, product_code QUH and applicant queries (https://api.fda.gov/device/510k.json) : used to find K numbers; each cited K number was then opened on accessdata.fda.gov.

## Heart health

### Shared
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32567342/ : Shimbo 2020 AHA/AMA statement; SMBP validated; validated oscillometric devices preferred; indications incl. white-coat/masked/morning hypertension; meta-analyses show lower BP; outcome RCTs needed.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23922064/ : Uhlig 2013, 52 studies; SMBP plus support lowers SBP 3.4 to 8.9 mmHg at 12 months; clinical outcome evidence insufficient.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32275193/ : Picone 2020; 18.3% of 278 upper-arm cuff devices validated; validated devices cost more.

- https://www.stridebp.org/bp-monitors : checked as an alternative list (not cited).
